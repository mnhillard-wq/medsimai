Before Your Encounter

Amira Kante — Nutrition Consult

A brief orientation before your MedSimAI session

What is MedSimAI?

MedSimAI is an AI clinical simulation platform for practicing patient encounters and talking through clinical reasoning with AI preceptors in a low-pressure learning setting.

1

Start the Case

Once you have been given access:

  1. Go to medsimai.com and log in.
  2. From the Student Dashboard, select Start a Case.
  3. Find your assigned course and select View Course Details.
  4. Open Amira Kante — Nutrition Consult.
  5. Review the instructions, door note, and available chart information before starting.
Best experience: Use a computer if possible and select Voice for the encounter.
2

Who You're Meeting

You will speak with Mariam, Amira's mother, who is providing the history for her 9-year-old daughter. Amira has an autism spectrum disorder.

Mariam can tell you what she has observed about Amira's eating, behavior, symptoms, and routines.

3

Taking the Nutrition History

Approach this as you would a real pediatric nutrition history.

A few tips:

  • Ask one question at a time.
  • Work through a typical day systematically, including meals, snacks, and drinks.
  • Clarify how much is actually consumed, not only what is offered.
  • Ask about foods that are refused or absent, not only preferred foods.
  • Explore feeding skills, safety, texture, and mealtime behavior as well as food choices.
  • Ask about current and previous supplements.
  • Keep the family's routines, resources, and realistic options in mind.

4

Use the Chart

Review the door note and EHR-style chart before and during the case as needed.

Use the history, growth information, examination findings, and laboratory results together as parts of the same clinical picture.

5

Meet Your Preceptor

After the history, you will work through the case with Dr. Ruth Adeyemi, a pediatric clinical nutrition specialist.

She will ask you questions about how you are thinking, including your nutritional concerns, interpretation of the findings, possible explanations, treatment, dietary recommendations, and follow-up.

This is a coached reasoning discussion, not a quiz. You do not need to have everything figured out before you begin. Explain what you think, why you think it, and where you are uncertain.
