Complete History Series

Four adult patients and a presentation coach. In each case the learner takes a complete history by voice or text with an AI-simulated patient, examines the patient, and writes a note, with structured feedback after each step. Encounters can be repeated.

What happens in each case
  1. Complete history. Interview the patient, then share an impression and next steps in plain language.
  2. Physical exam. Choose exam maneuvers and see this patient's findings.
  3. Written note. History, exam, problem representation, and a differential with supporting evidence.
