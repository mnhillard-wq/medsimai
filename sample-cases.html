MedSimAI Sample Cases

Sample MedSimAI cases arranged by learner level. Each entry lists the activities in order, the skills practiced, and the reasoning the case is built to draw out. Every encounter ends with rubric feedback; several continue to an AI attending, nurse or consultant.

Skills at a glance

A filled dot marks a skill the case assesses; an open dot marks one it exercises without scoring.

Levels reflect the learners each case was written for; faculty can place any case earlier or later. Feedback is AI-generated against faculty rubrics; human calibration and faculty sign-off are completed before student use.
